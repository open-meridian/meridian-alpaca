# Alpaca's terms

A summary, not legal advice. Read Alpaca's own terms before relying on it.

- Alpaca's market data is for **personal, non-commercial use**: a
  non-professional subscriber receives it solely for their own personal,
  non-business use.
- **No redistribution.** Nothing may be republished or redistributed without
  Alpaca's written consent.
- **What a personal deployment may keep:** its own copy, for the account
  holder's use.

So the plugin declares each dataset's terms as one person's. Your
deployment's Data sources page records the licence you enter, and warns
when more than one person can read a plugin entitled to a one-person
dataset. Open Meridian does not decide whether your use meets Alpaca's
terms; the licence records what you entered.

An agent connected to your deployment may pass what it reads to its model's
host; whether that is allowed is for Alpaca's terms to say.
